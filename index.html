Word and PowerPoint to H5P Converter

Build an H5P Course Presentation from a Word or PowerPoint form, in three steps. Your file stays on your computer.

Step 1: Download a template

Choose whichever you prefer. Both give you the same lesson structure, and PowerPoint gives you more control over how slides look.

Word template PowerPoint template

Keep an eye on this page. I'll keep refreshing the templates with new styles and layouts.

Step 2: Fill it in and save it

Replace the sample text and add your pictures. You can copy, move or delete slides. For each answer, use the Correct or Wrong label to mark it.

Step 3: Convert it

Drop your saved template into the box below. Your H5P file will download, ready to upload to H5P, where you can also add your videos.

Good to know

  • The converter only reads these templates, not ordinary documents or slide decks.
  • In PowerPoint, text size, colour, bold, italics and underline carry across, though sizes may look slightly different in H5P.
  • To change a PowerPoint slide's colour, use Format Background and choose a solid fill. On a coloured slide, also tick Hide Background Graphics.
  • In Word, only the words and pictures are used.
